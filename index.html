Guess the Pitch

  • The pitch leaves the hand and freezes at your decision point, the last moment you can still choose to swing.
  • Press Press here to start. The pitcher goes through the same delivery every pitch, so load with him.
  • Two pitch names show up, one on the LEFT and one on the RIGHT. Tap the side you think it is (or use the ← → keys).
  • The rest of the flight plays out, then you see if you were right and the tell.
  • 10 pitches per level. Your reaction time is tracked.
  • Works best full screen, held either way.

Pitch flight comes from each pitcher's real 2026 Statcast averages (Baseball Savant). 13U and HS versions are scaled models (estimate), not real youth pitches. Decision point: about 150–175 ms before the plate for HS, D1 and MLB, set at 165 ms here. 13U uses 200 ms, an estimate for slower bats. View is from the hitter's eyes. The pitcher figure is drawn about 2x life size so you can see his delivery; the ball still leaves from his real release point. The delivery is the same length every pitch (a timing cue, not his real tempo). Tells are coaching philosophy.
